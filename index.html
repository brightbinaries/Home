    Questions we get a lot

    Can I cancel hosting and take my site with me?

    Yes. The site is yours. If you leave, we hand over the files and help you move them.

    Do I need my own domain name?

    No. If you don’t have one, we’ll help you pick and register it. If you already own one, we connect it for free.

    What if I need a change after launch?

    Send us a message. Each plan includes a set number of updates every month, and extra ones are billed at a fixed small rate.

    Do you work with large businesses?

    Yes. Enterprise projects get a dedicated contact, custom features and a written timeline before we start.

    How do I pay?

    The build fee is split in two: half to start, half at launch. Hosting is billed monthly or yearly.
