Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our process, code ownership, and care plans.

Can I cancel hosting later and take my website with me?

Yes, absolutely. You retain 100% ownership of your website design, code, assets, and content. If you ever decide to transition hosting to another provider or an internal server, we export your clean code files and help you move them without lock-in.

Do I need to purchase a domain name before getting started?

Not at all. If you already have a domain, we connect it seamlessly at launch for free. If you do not have one yet, we assist in finding, purchasing, and configuring the ideal .com domain name for your company.

What if I need changes or updates after the site is launched?

That is the best part of our studio care subscription! Just shoot us a quick email or text with the edits you need (new photos, text changes, staff bios, or announcements). Minor updates are handled within 24 hours under your monthly plan allowance.

How are payments split, and is there a satisfaction guarantee?

The one-time build fee is split into two fair payments: 50% to initiate the kickoff and reserve your build window, and 50% only when you review and approve the final site for launch. We also provide a 30-day post-launch warranty with free fixes.

Do you work with custom enterprise architectures and web apps?

Yes. Our Enterprise tier is tailored for multi-region organizations, SaaS brands, and portals requiring custom API integrations (Stripe, HubSpot, Salesforce, Supabase), SSO authentication, and strict SOC2/GDPR compliance guidelines.
